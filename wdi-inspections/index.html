Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a WDI inspection required to sell a house in Texas?+
A WDI inspection is not legally required by Texas law, but is required by most mortgage lenders — including FHA, VA, and conventional loans. Even in cash transactions, most buyers request one as standard due diligence in Southeast Texas.
What does a WDI inspection look for?+
Our WDI inspectors look for evidence of subterranean termites, drywood termites, carpenter ants, and wood-boring beetles in all accessible areas including the foundation, interior visible wood, attic, and exterior.
How long does a WDI inspection take?+
A WDI inspection for a typical Southeast Texas home takes 30–60 minutes. Reports are delivered promptly after the inspection — often same day.
What happens if the WDI inspection finds termites?+
If we find active wood-destroying insects, we document the species, location, and extent of infestation. We can provide a treatment proposal and, upon completion, issue a clearance letter to satisfy your lender's requirements.
