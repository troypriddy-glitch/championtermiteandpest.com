How do I get rid of termites in Southeast Texas?+
Southeast Texas has some of the highest subterranean termite pressure in the country. Effective treatment requires a professional inspection to determine the extent of infestation, followed by either a liquid barrier treatment, an in-ground baiting system, or a combination of both. Store-bought products are ineffective against established colonies. Call 409-209-2091 for a free termite inspection.
What is a WDI inspection and do I need one to sell my house in Texas?+
A WDI (Wood-Destroying Insect) inspection documents the presence or absence of termites, carpenter ants, wood-boring beetles, and carpenter bees. While not legally required by Texas law, most mortgage lenders — including FHA, VA, and conventional loans — require a WDI report before closing. We offer fast same-day and next-day WDI inspections throughout Southeast Texas.

Pricing depends on the type of service, the size of your property, and the severity of the pest problem. We provide free no-obligation estimates for all services. Call 409-209-2091 or contact us online for a free quote — we offer competitive pricing and no long-term contracts.
Is Champion Termite & Pest Control licensed in Texas?+
Yes. Champion Termite & Pest Control is fully licensed by the Texas Department of Agriculture Structural Pest Control Service and is fully insured for residential and commercial pest control operations throughout Southeast Texas. We are also A+ accredited with the Better Business Bureau since 2020.
What is the best mosquito control for Southeast Texas?+
For Southeast Texas's high-humidity, near-year-round mosquito season, we recommend monthly barrier spray treatments during peak season (April through October). The barrier spray kills adult mosquitoes on contact and continues working for 21-30 days. Regular monthly treatments provide continuous protection throughout Southeast Texas mosquito season.
