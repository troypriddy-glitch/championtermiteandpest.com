Southeast Texas's warm climate, coastal proximity, and abundance of food sources make it prime territory for roof rats and Norway rats. Rodents contaminate food, damage electrical wiring, compromise structural integrity, and carry diseases. A single pair of rats can produce up to 2,000 offspring in a year — early intervention is critical.

What We Offer

Rodent Control Solutions

01

Thorough Inspection

Every rodent program begins with a comprehensive inspection of your attic, crawl space, exterior, and interior to identify entry points, nesting sites, runways, and the extent of the infestation.

02

Bait Stations & Trapping

Tamper-resistant bait stations and professional traps placed at key locations inside and outside your home provide rapid population reduction.

03

Structural Exclusion

We seal every gap, crack, and opening rodents use to enter — including roofline gaps, pipe penetrations, foundation vents, and utility entries. Without exclusion, rodents will return regardless of how much bait you set.

04

Attic Remediation

After elimination, we assess attic insulation for contamination. Rat urine and droppings in insulation create health hazards and odor problems. We offer clean-out and insulation replacement services.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if I have rats or mice in my Southeast Texas home?+
Common signs include droppings, gnaw marks, scratching sounds in walls or ceilings at night, grease marks along walls, and nesting material in attics or crawl spaces. Roof rats are particularly common throughout Southeast Texas.
Can I get rid of rats myself with store-bought products?+
Store-bought products can reduce populations temporarily but rarely eliminate an infestation. Without sealing all entry points, new rodents will replace those you kill within days. Professional exclusion combined with targeted bait placement is the only long-term solution.
How long does rodent control take?+
Initial population knockdown typically occurs within 1–2 weeks. Full exclusion is usually completed in 1–3 service visits depending on severity. We offer follow-up inspections to verify success.
Do you offer a guarantee on rodent control?+
Yes. Our rodent exclusion program is backed by our satisfaction guarantee. If rodents re-enter through any sealed point, we return to re-treat at no additional charge.
